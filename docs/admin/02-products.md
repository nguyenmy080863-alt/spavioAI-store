# Products

Open **Products** in the sidebar.

## List
- Search by name, SKU or category and filter by status (published, draft, archived, preorder).
- Select rows to **archive**, **restore** or permanently **delete** in bulk.
- **Publish / Unpublish** toggles a product on the storefront.
- Pages show 8 products at a time.

## Create or edit
Use **New product** on the Products page (`/admin/products/new`) or click a product name. You can set name, SKU, category, description, price and sale price, stock and low-stock threshold, images, and preorder options (deposit and expected shipping date).

## Images
Drag and drop several images at once. They are compressed, thumbnails are generated, and files are stored in a private bucket.

## Permissions
Only **Super Admin** and **Inventory Manager** can change products. Other roles can view.

## Stock
The stock number in the product form is the quantity on hand. Changing it is saved as a manual adjustment in the inventory ledger. For adjustments with a reason, use **Inventory**; stock also changes through purchase orders and orders. See the Inventory page for how on hand, incoming, committed and available relate.



Every change to products is recorded in the **Audit log**.
