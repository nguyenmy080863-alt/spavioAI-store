# Payment link

If we took your order by phone or in person, we may send you a **payment link**. You do not need an account.

1. Open the link. It shows your items, any discount, shipping and the total (prices include VAT).
2. Check or fill in your **delivery address** and phone number.
3. Pay securely with **PayPal**.
4. You see your **order number**. We confirm your payment shortly and you can follow your order under **Track my order** with the order number and your email.

Good to know:
- The link is personal. Do not share it. It is valid for a limited time; if it has expired, ask us for a new one.
- Opening the link again never creates a second order. If you already started paying, the same order is used.
- If something does not match what we agreed, do not pay: contact us.
