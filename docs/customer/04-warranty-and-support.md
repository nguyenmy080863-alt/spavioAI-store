# Warranty and support

Every device has a **2-year warranty against manufacturing defects**, counted from the delivery date. You do not need an account to ask for help.

## Open a ticket
1. Open **Warranty** in the footer (`/warranty`).
2. Choose what you need: **guidance** (setup, usage, care), a **product error or defect**, or something else.
3. Enter your name and email, and the product. Add your **order number** (for example `SO-00042`) so we can confirm your warranty, and the **serial number** if you have it.
4. Describe the problem: what happens, since when and what you already tried.
5. Send the ticket. You get a **ticket number** such as `WT-00001`. Keep it with your email address.

If the order number and email do not match an order, the ticket is still accepted and our team checks your purchase by hand.

## Follow your ticket
- **Guests:** open **Track your ticket** (`/warranty/track`) and enter the ticket number and the email you used.
- **Signed-in customers:** your tickets are listed on `/account`. Tickets you open while signed in are linked to your account.

The ticket page shows the status, your warranty standing, a scheduled visit (if any), the resolution and the conversation. Reply there while the ticket is not closed. Replying to a resolved ticket reopens it.

## Statuses
Open, In review, Waiting for your reply, Visit scheduled, Resolved, Closed.

## Good to know
- We do not send emails about tickets yet. Check the ticket page for replies.
- You can open at most 5 tickets per email per day. Reply to an existing ticket for follow-up questions.
- Photos and videos cannot be attached yet; describe the problem in words.
