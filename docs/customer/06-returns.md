# Returns and exchanges

You can try your device for **30 days** from delivery. If it is not right for you, return it for a refund. You do not need an account.

## Start a return
1. Open **Returns** in the footer (`/returns`), or press **Return items** on your orders page.
2. Enter your **order number** and the **email** you ordered with.
3. Choose the items and quantities, choose **Refund** or **Exchange for another device**, and tell us why. For an exchange pick the replacement device for each item; you see the price difference straight away.
4. Confirm that the device and accessories are complete and in the original packaging.
5. Send the request. You get a **return number** such as `RT-00001`. Keep it with your email address.

You can return an item once, within 30 days after it was delivered. Orders that are unpaid or cancelled cannot be returned. If the device is faulty, you can also use the warranty.

## What happens next
| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Requested | We are reviewing your request |
| Approved | Send the items back as described in our message. If we provide a **prepaid label**, download it on your return page, print it and stick it on the parcel. You can add the tracking number there |
| Received | We received the parcel and are checking it |
| Refunded (or *Completed* for an exchange) | The money is on its way back to your original payment method, or your replacement is being prepared |
| Rejected | We could not accept it; our message explains why |
| Cancelled | You or we cancelled the request |

Follow the return at `/returns/track` with the return number and email, or from your orders page. You can cancel a request while it is still *Requested*.

## Exchanges
After we receive and check your parcel we create the replacement order. If the replacement costs more, we contact you to arrange the difference before we ship it. If it costs less, we refund the difference. Replacements must be in stock.

## Refunds
- The refund covers the items. Shipping costs are not refunded.
- If we send you a prepaid return label, we pay for it. Where the return page says the label cost is deducted from your refund, that applies (never for defective devices or wrong items).
- It goes to your original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the return.
- Used brush heads and attachments are replaced before resale for hygiene reasons.
- Your statutory 14-day right of withdrawal is not affected.

You receive an email at every step once emails are switched on; until then check your return page for updates.
