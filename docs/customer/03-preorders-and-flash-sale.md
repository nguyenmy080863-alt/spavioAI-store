# Preorders and flash sale

## Preorders
Some products can be ordered before they ship.
- The product page shows a **Preorder** badge and the expected shipping date.
- Customers pay a **deposit** at checkout (a fixed amount or a percentage). The bag and checkout show *due today* and *due at shipping*.
- The balance is paid when the item ships.

> Planned: charging the balance automatically when a preorder ships (needs order records).

## Flash sale countdown
A flash sale shows a **countdown timer** and a progress bar of how much has "sold".
- The timer starts when the visitor first sees it and survives page reloads.
- When it ends it resets according to the rule set in the admin (loop or delay).
- The sale prices and the stock/sold bar are display only; **checkout prices do not change**.
