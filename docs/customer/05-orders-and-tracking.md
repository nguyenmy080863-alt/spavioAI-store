# Orders and tracking

## Your orders (with an account)
Sign in and open **Your orders** on `/account` (`/account/orders`). For every order you see:
- a simple **status**: awaiting payment, payment being checked, preparing, shipped, delivered, cancelled or refunded,
- the items, shipping, and any preorder balance still due at shipping,
- the **delivery** with carrier and **tracking number** once it ships,
- the **warranty end date** (2 years from delivery),
- **Buy again**, **Report a problem** (opens a warranty ticket with the order and product already filled in) and a general help link.

Orders you placed earlier as a guest with the same email show up too, once that email is confirmed on your account.

## Track an order (without an account)
Open **Track my order** in the footer (`/orders/track`) and enter your **order number** and the **email** you ordered with. You see the same details as above. The link in your order emails opens this page for you.

## Order emails
You receive an email when your order is received, when your payment is confirmed and when it ships. Emails are not switched on yet; until then use the pages above.

## Statuses explained
| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Awaiting payment | We have not received a payment for this order |
| Payment being checked | You paid; we are checking the payment |
| Preparing | Payment confirmed, we are getting the order ready |
| Shipped | At least one parcel is with the carrier |
| Delivered | Everything arrived; the warranty has started |
